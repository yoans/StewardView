How we operate StewardView

Import. Categorize. Assign a fund.

That is the weekly work. I import the bank file and the Givelify file. Then each transaction gets a budget category and a fund.

Category

What kind of receipt or bill it is. Offering, preacher salary, electric, mission support, office supplies, and the other lines from our usual workbook.

Fund

Where the money sits. General for ordinary operations. A purpose fund when the gift or spend belongs there.

StewardView

The budget

Built from how we already spend

The monthly budget is a forecast of regular items: payroll, utilities, insurance, mission support, and the rest of the usual month. It is there so we can see a surprise early, and so we can talk about the year with the same list in front of us.

  • Regular bills stay on the budget.
  • A special expense gets its own fund. A roof, a van, a one-time project.
  • A regular special item also gets a fund. Something we do every year that is not the ordinary month, like LTC.

Funds

I need your help on recurring fund gifts

I can import and categorize. What I cannot do alone is keep purpose funds funded. If we want money set aside for missions, building, benevolence, youth, or a coming project, people have to give to those funds on a regular basis.

  • Givelify envelopes map to funds. Unmatched gifts go to General.
  • If we spend from a purpose fund, we say so. That money was given for a reason.
  • I am asking you to help drive recurring contributions into those funds, in the congregation and in this room.

Who does what

I run the tool. You use it to agree and plan.

What I do

Import the bank and Givelify files. Categorize. Assign funds. Keep the monthly budget current. Fix a mismatch if funds and checking disagree.

What you do

Log in with View access. Look at Dashboard, Budget, and Funds before we meet. Ask questions from the same numbers.

When we decide

Look at the category and the fund on the screen. Confirm we have cash, we have plan room, and we are using the right fund.

What I need from you

Help people give regularly into the purpose funds. That is how we pay for special work without starving the lights.
