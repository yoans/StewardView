Earmarked funds

Funds answer: “Is this money already promised to a purpose?”

Categories classify activity. Earmarked funds hold savings and gifts that belong to a specific cause — building and maintenance, youth, the needy, outreach, fellowship, and emergencies.

Step 1
Someone gives

Online (Givelify) or otherwise, often toward an envelope or campaign.

Step 2
We earmark

The gift is mapped to the right fund so it isn’t mixed into unrestricted cash.

Step 3
You can see it

Fund balances and history show what we can spend for that purpose.

How we fill them

Name it. Set it aside. Spend only from it.

Step 1
Create the funds

Treasurer confirms each named fund is open in StewardView so every gift and set-aside has a home.

Step 2
Fill them on purpose

Directed giving (Givelify envelopes) plus monthly transfers from the budget or surplus.

Step 3
Spend from the right purse

Elders approve against the fund that owns the purpose — building, youth, needy, outreach, game night, or emergency.

You will see the balances. Before a meeting, open Funds. If a fund is thin, that is the conversation — not a surprise at the moment of need.

StewardView

Contributions

Watch how giving is directed

StewardView tracks contributions — including Givelify imports — and shows whether gifts went to the General Fund or to a directed / earmarked fund.

  • General giving supports the operating budget (Tithes & Offerings)
  • Directed giving fills a named fund — building, youth, needy, outreach, game night, or emergency
  • Unmapped gifts default to the General Fund so nothing is lost

This is how we honor donor intent and avoid spending restricted gifts on something else.

Budget oversight

Budget vs. Actual is your monthly scoreboard

Leadership builds the plan. StewardView shows how we’re tracking — by category, month, and year-to-date.

Ask here

“Can the building fund cover this repair?” “Is youth funded for camp?” “Do we have enough in emergency to get through a thin month?”

Decide with facts

Approve spending against available budget and available earmarked balances — not guesswork.

How you’ll use it

A simple rhythm for elders & trustees

  • Before a meeting — open Budget and Funds; note which named funds are healthy and which are thin
  • During discussion — pull up the category or earmarked fund on screen
  • When approving spend — confirm: budget room + the correct fund (building, youth, needy, outreach, game night, emergency)
  • Afterward — leave the bookkeeping to treasurer/admin; keep asking for clarity

View access = informed voice. You steward decisions; the system holds the detail.
